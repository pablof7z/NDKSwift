This visualization shows how data flows through NDKSwift when your app creates a subscription. Click any node to see detailed information about that module - what it receives, what it produces, and what its responsibilities are. Press Play to watch an animated walkthrough of the entire subscription lifecycle, from your app requesting data all the way to events arriving from Nostr relays.
